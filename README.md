# Activity-2
# _From Information to Visual Communication
_
## Description

This activity presents a two-page professional infographic about Mechanical Engineering and its role in society. The infographic was designed using visual elements such as icons, images, typography, color, hierarchy, and progress rings to communicate information in a clear and engaging way.


## Page 1: Impact of Mechanical Engineering on Society

The first page introduces Mechanical Engineering as a field that combines science, mathematics, design, and technology to develop machines, systems, and practical solutions that improve everyday life. It highlights the field's impact on industry and manufacturing, transportation, energy, and sustainability. It also discusses why Mechanical Engineering matters and presents emerging areas such as automation, robotics, renewable energy, smart manufacturing, and advanced materials.


## Page 2: Who Chooses Mechanical Engineering?

The second page presents a graphical comparison of students from four selected programs: Mechanical Engineering, Architecture, Industrial Engineering, and Civil Engineering. Using progress rings, the infographic shows the percentage distribution of the 1,346 students represented in the data, with Mechanical Engineering accounting for 8.92% or 120 students. The page also summarizes the number of students in each program and emphasizes how different fields contribute to shaping society and the future.


## Design and Visual Communication

The infographic uses a consistent visual theme, combining pink and white colors, engineering-related images, gear icons, rounded sections, bold headings, and graphical data representations. The layout was organized to create a clear visual hierarchy and make the information easy to understand while maintaining a professional and modern appearance.


## Conclusion

This infographic demonstrates how information can be transformed into an engaging visual presentation. It highlights the importance of Mechanical Engineering while showing how different academic fields contribute to innovation, development, and the improvement of society.
